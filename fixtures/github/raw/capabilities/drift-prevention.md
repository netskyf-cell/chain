# Capability: drift-prevention

> Product builders and teams can follow the AI SDLC and avoid drift between vision and execution.

## Why this matters

Plans and outcomes diverge quietly. A requirement changes after build starts, a subtask is dropped, a gate is skipped. Teams find out in review, or in production. Surfacing the divergence while it is still cheap to correct is the main reason this product exists.

## What done looks like (delivery-based, v1)

All features linked to this capability have shipped. Drift signals are deterministic, each carries a link to its evidence, and each can be dismissed with a reason.
